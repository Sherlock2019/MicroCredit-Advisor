# Smallholder Maize and Bean Plan - BRW-MW0412

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Pseudonymous borrower; all figures fictional.

## Farm activities
The household plants 2 hectares of maize and half a hectare of beans at the start of the rains in November. The maize harvest is expected in April and beans in May.

## Household cash flow
Outside the farm, the household earns about 60 USD per month from casual labour. Household living costs are about 120 USD per month. Cash is tightest between January and March, before the maize harvest, when food purchases rise.

## Requested loan
The borrower requests 1,500 USD for seed and fertilizer, to be repaid in ten equal monthly instalments starting the month after disbursement.
