# Farmer Cooperative A - Mill Delivery Records 2023-2025

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Fictional delivery summaries used for a software demonstration.

## Main-season deliveries
In the 2023 main season the cooperative delivered 39.2 tonnes of paddy from 10 hectares to the mill. In 2024 it delivered 41.0 tonnes and in 2025 it delivered 39.8 tonnes. The three-season average yield is 4.0 tonnes per hectare.

## Prices received
The average farm-gate paddy price paid by the mill was 410 USD per tonne in 2023, 362 USD per tonne in 2024 and 405 USD per tonne in 2025. Payment was made within 21 days of delivery.

## Dry-season history
The cooperative has not yet grown a dry-season crop collectively. Two members grew dry-season rice individually in 2025 on less than one hectare each, and no delivery records exist for those plots.
