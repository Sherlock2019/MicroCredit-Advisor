# Farmer Cooperative A - Rice Production and Investment Plan 2026-27

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT prepared for a software demonstration. The cooperative, its members and all figures are fictional.

## Cooperative profile
Farmer Cooperative A groups 46 smallholder rice growers in the Mwea irrigation area. Members cultivate 10 hectares of paddy under the cooperative's collective plan. The cooperative has operated since 2019 and markets paddy collectively to two licensed mills.

## Production plan
The main season covers all 10 hectares, transplanted in May and harvested in August 2026. The cooperative plans a smaller dry-season crop on 5 hectares where water allocation allows, harvested in February 2027. Main-season yields over the last three seasons averaged 4.0 tonnes per hectare according to mill delivery records. Dry-season yield of 3.0 tonnes per hectare is a projection based on a neighbouring scheme.

## Use of the loan
The cooperative requests 12,000 USD. About 9,000 USD will finance land preparation, certified seed, fertilizer, transplanting labour and dry-season inputs. A further 3,000 USD buys a small shared thresher, which members expect to reduce post-harvest losses and hired threshing costs.

## Repayment proposal
The cooperative proposes two seasonal instalments: the first after the main-season harvest in August 2026 and the second after the dry-season harvest in February 2027. Paddy will be sold at the farm-gate price agreed with the mill, assumed at 400 USD per tonne.

## Existing obligations
The cooperative repays an equipment loan of 200 USD per month until mid-2027. Monthly administrative costs of about 150 USD cover the store keeper and record keeping.

## Risks identified by the cooperative
Water allocation for the dry season is not guaranteed and depends on the scheme's rotation schedule. Paddy prices fell by around 12 percent in the 2024 glut. The cooperative proposes storing part of the harvest in its warehouse to avoid selling at the lowest prices.
