# Vegetable Producer Group N - Winter Season Plan

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.

## Plan
Twelve members of the producer group will grow tomato and cauliflower on 3 hectares in Chitwan during the winter season. Drip irrigation kits will reduce water use and labour for watering. Tomatoes are harvested from January and cauliflower from April.
