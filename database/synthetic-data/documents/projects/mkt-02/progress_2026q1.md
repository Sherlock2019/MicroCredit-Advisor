# Smallholder Market Access Project - Progress Report 2026-Q1

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.

## Progress
The Adama aggregation centre opened in February 2026 and handled 1,200 tonnes of teff and wheat in its first two months. Feeder road works near Hawassa reached 18 of 30 kilometres. Implementation is broadly on schedule.

## Producer groups
Eighty-two producer groups have business plans, and 47 have signed seasonal supply agreements with buyers.
