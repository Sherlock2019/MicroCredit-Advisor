# Rural Irrigation Resilience Programme - Quarterly Progress Report 2026-Q1

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Figures are fictional.

## Farmers reached
By the end of March 2026, 6,200 farmers had received at least one programme service, against an end-of-project target of 10,000. The beneficiary register has been de-duplicated for Mwea and Ahero only; figures for the other three sites may contain double counting.

## Water user associations
Fourteen water user associations hold regular elections and collect maintenance fees. Registration of the remaining six associations is delayed because county registration offices suspended processing during the first quarter.

## Farmer field schools
Farmer field schools continued at four sites. Attendance sheets from the implementing partner report 2,900 farmers completing a full season, but these sheets have not yet been reconciled with the beneficiary register.

## Issues
The project management unit notes that phase-one construction is behind schedule and that the quarterly report for 2025-Q4 was not submitted because the M&E officer post was vacant from November to January.
