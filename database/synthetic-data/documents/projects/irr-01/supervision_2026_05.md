# Rural Irrigation Resilience Programme - Supervision Mission Report May 2026

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. The mission, its members and findings are fictional.

## Construction progress
The mission visited nine schemes. Seven schemes are certified complete and deliver water to 1,450 hectares. Phase-one construction stands at about 40 percent against 70 percent planned. The main causes of delay are late contractor mobilisation at three schemes, heavy rains between October and December 2025, and a six-month delay in procuring PVC pipes after a failed tender.

## Phase two
Phase-two construction has not started. Tender documents are ready but cannot be launched until the procurement plan revision is approved.

## Financial management
Contract commitments for infrastructure have reached 2.3 million USD while disbursements stand at 1.65 million USD. The mission cautions that cost per hectare is running above the design estimate because of price escalation in cement and pipes.

## Agreed actions
The project will submit a construction recovery plan with revised dates by 31 July 2026, recruit a procurement specialist, submit the missing 2025-Q4 progress report and the 2025-H2 financial report, and reconcile farmer field school attendance with the beneficiary register before the next quarterly report.
