# Rural Irrigation Resilience Programme - Detailed Design Report

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT for a software demonstration. Not an IFAD document.

## Scope of design
The design covers 20 small-scale irrigation schemes in five counties, grouped into a first phase of 12 schemes and a second phase of 8 schemes. Detailed designs, bills of quantities and environmental screening were completed for all 12 phase-one schemes in April 2025.

## Design standards
Canals are lined where seepage losses exceed 30 percent. Each scheme includes a farmer-managed intake, flow measurement structures and a maintenance plan agreed with the water user association.

## Cost estimate
The engineering estimate for phase one is 1.9 million USD and for phase two 1.1 million USD, within the 3.0 million USD infrastructure component.
